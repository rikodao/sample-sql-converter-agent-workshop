   - PostgreSQL NUMERIC(19,4): 4 decimal places, larger range

2. DATEDIFF Year Calculation:
   - SQL Server: DATEDIFF(yy, a, b) counts year boundaries crossed

   - Impact: MINOR (may differ by 1 for dates near year boundary)

              extract(year from age('2024-01-01', '2023-12-31')) = 0 in PG
   - Mitigation: For this use case (hire_date to fiscal year end), difference is acceptable

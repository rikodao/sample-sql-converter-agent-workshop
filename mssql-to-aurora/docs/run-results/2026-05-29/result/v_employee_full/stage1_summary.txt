INFERRED VIEW STRUCTURE
============================================================================
CREATE VIEW dbo.v_employee_full
AS
SELECT 
    e.employee_id,
    e.first_name,
    e.last_name,
    e.email,
    e.phone_number,
    e.hire_date,
    e.job_id,
    e.salary,
    e.commission_pct,
    e.manager_id,
    e.department_id,
    e.status,
    e.termination_date,
    e.is_active,
    e.performance_rating,
    e.bonus_amount,
    e.last_bonus_date,
    e.bonus_fiscal_year,
    d.department_name,
    d.location,
    d.manager_id AS dept_manager_id
FROM dbo.employees e
LEFT JOIN dbo.departments d ON e.department_id = d.department_id;

Key Features:
- LEFT JOIN ensures all employees shown (even without department)
- Combines employee and department data
- Used by trg_v_employee_full_update (INSTEAD OF UPDATE trigger)
- No WHERE clause (shows all employees, unlike v_active_employees)
